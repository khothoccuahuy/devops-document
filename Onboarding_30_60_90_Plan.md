## 4.1 Purpose

This section guides new DevOps team members through their first 90 days. The goal is to ensure every new hire is productive, confident, and well-integrated into the team by the end of their third month.

---

## 4.2 Before Day 1 (Manager Checklist)

- [ ] Create accounts: GitHub, AWS/GCP, Jira, Confluence, Slack, PagerDuty
- [ ] Add to relevant Slack channels (`#devops`, `#incidents`, `#deployments`, `#alerts`)
- [ ] Assign an onboarding buddy (senior engineer)
- [ ] Share this handbook link and Section 1
- [ ] Schedule 1:1 with DevOps Lead for Day 1
- [ ] Prepare laptop and required access credentials

---

## 4.3 Day 1 Checklist (New Hire)

- [ ] Meet the team — attend daily standup
- [ ] Set up local development environment (see Section 20 for tools)
- [ ] Clone key repositories and verify access
- [ ] Read Section 1 (Introduction) and Section 2 (Roles)
- [ ] Join all required Slack channels
- [ ] 1:1 with DevOps Lead — align on 30/60/90 expectations

---

## 4.4 The 30/60/90 Day Plan

### 📅 First 30 Days — Learn & Observe
**Goal:** Understand the systems, the team, and the culture.

- [ ] Complete reading of this entire handbook
- [ ] Shadow 3+ deployments end-to-end
- [ ] Attend all team meetings for 4 weeks
- [ ] Set up and explore all key tools (see Section 20)
- [ ] Read existing runbooks (Section 13)
- [ ] Pair with buddy on at least 2 tasks
- [ ] Complete onboarding certification (AWS Associate or equivalent)
- [ ] Deliver: Short summary of what you've learned to the team

### 📅 Days 31–60 — Contribute & Build
**Goal:** Start owning tasks and contributing independently.

- [ ] Take ownership of 1–2 Jira tickets independently
- [ ] Make at least 1 infrastructure improvement (small scope)
- [ ] Write or update at least 1 runbook
- [ ] Join on-call rotation (shadowing first)
- [ ] Complete first code review on an IaC PR
- [ ] Schedule 1:1 with each team member
- [ ] Deliver: First solo deployment to staging environment

### 📅 Days 61–90 — Own & Improve
**Goal:** Operate independently and propose improvements.

- [ ] Lead at least 1 full deployment cycle to production
- [ ] Participate in an incident response (or tabletop drill)
- [ ] Propose 1 improvement to process, tooling, or documentation
- [ ] Present a short tech-share to the team
- [ ] Complete 90-day review with manager
- [ ] Deliver: Written 90-day retrospective (what went well, what to improve)
